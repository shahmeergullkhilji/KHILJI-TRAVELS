About New Khilji

Our Story & Vision

12+
Years of Excellence

Pakistan's Trusted International Travel Partner Since 2012

New Khilji Travels & Tours was established in 2012 with a singular vision: to make world-class travel accessible to every Pakistani. Founded by travel enthusiast and entrepreneur Hafiz Tahir Khilji, we began as a small Umrah consultancy in Gujranwala and have grown into one of Pakistan's most trusted full-service travel agencies.

Today, we serve over 15,000 travellers annually across Pakistan, offering an extensive range of services from Umrah & Hajj pilgrimages to luxury international tours, visa consultancy, corporate travel management, and domestic adventures in our beautiful homeland.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver exceptional travel experiences that exceed expectations through personalized service, expert knowledge, and unwavering commitment to client satisfaction.

🌟 Our Vision

To become Pakistan's most trusted and innovative travel company, connecting hearts to the world's most extraordinary destinations.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a package with New Khilji Travels?

Booking is simple! You can call us, visit our office, fill the online booking form, or message us on WhatsApp. Our consultants will guide you through every step including package selection, payment, and documentation.

Are your Umrah packages ATOL/IATA certified?

Yes, we are IATA certified and fully registered with PTDC (Pakistan Tourism Development Corporation). All our Umrah & Hajj operations comply with Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Hajj requirements.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfers (all major Pakistani banks), Easy Paisa, Jazz Cash, and installment plans for select packages. Contact us to discuss flexible payment options.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes, we offer comprehensive travel insurance through our partner insurers covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, baggage loss, and travel delays for all international packages.

Can I customize my tour package?

Absolutely! All our packages are fully customizable. Tell us your budget, dates, preferences, and interests, and our consultants will create a bespoke itinerary just for you.

Process

Our Visa Process

1

Free Initial Consultation

Meet or call our certified visa consultants who evaluate your profile, travel history, and recommend the most suitable visa category and country-specific strategy.

2

Document Collection & Review

We provide a complete checklist of required documents and thoroughly review each document for accuracy, completeness, and compliance with embassy requirements.

3

Application Preparation & Submission

Our experts prepare a compelling visa application package and submit it to the respective embassy or consulate with proper tracking and follow-up.

4

Appointment Scheduling

We handle all embassy appointment scheduling, biometric appointments, and coordinate any interview preparation sessions required.

5

Visa Collection & Travel Ready

Upon approval, we hand-deliver your passport with visa and provide complete pre-departure guidance, including travel insurance and packing tips.
